Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What this app is

NuffSaid Poster is a private publishing tool. It is used only by the owner of the NuffSaid accounts (Instagram @nuffsaid.bctalks and TikTok @nuffsaid.bc.talks) to upload that owner's own videos and captions to those accounts at a scheduled time. It has no other users and no sign up.

What data it uses

  • Access granted by the account owner when they log in with Instagram or TikTok: basic profile information (account ID, username) and permission to upload or publish videos to their own account.
  • The owner's own video files and captions, read from the owner's own cloud storage.
  • Results of each upload (post link, time, success or error), so the owner can see what was published.

The app does not collect data about other people, followers or viewers, and does not read messages.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to publish to the owner's accounts. Videos are downloaded only for the time needed to upload them and are then deleted. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in Instagram (Settings, Apps and websites) or TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services). To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the contact below.
